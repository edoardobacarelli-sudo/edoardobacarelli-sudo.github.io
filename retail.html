OperationsIn progress

Online retail sales & cancellation records

Assess recorded sales and cancellation patterns across products and countries.

Excel first · SQL and Power BI planned

Project scope

A self-directed learning project using the public UCI Online Retail dataset, independent of my employment at Fenix. It contains 541,909 invoice-line records from December 2010 to December 2011, with prices in GBP. The final month is partial. The historical data does not describe current market conditions.

View UCI source · CC BY 4.0 →

Status & limitations

No verified findings are published yet. C-prefixed records are not automatically physical returns. Signed transaction value is not automatically net revenue. Profit, inventory availability and delivery performance cannot be established from this dataset alone.

Operation checklist

Expand each operation to inspect its intended scope. These are planned steps, not completed results.

1Preserve and inspect the source+

Keep the raw source intact and inspect available fields and their meaning.

2Audit record quality+

Check data types, missing values, duplicates and the signs of quantities and prices. Document exclusions.

3Define invoices and metrics+

Specify qualifying invoices, sales value, signed transaction value and recorded cancellation measures, including denominators.

4Prepare analytical aggregates+

Build Excel summaries, with SQL as a planned extension, at suitable invoice, product, country and time levels.

5Reconcile results+

Check totals against the audited source. Distinguish lines from invoices and avoid misleading partial-month comparisons.

6Build the commercial overview+

Select charts and tables that communicate validated patterns without unsupported profit or stock claims.

7Document priorities and limits+

Write a concise decision memo identifying investigation priorities, evidence and unresolved questions.
