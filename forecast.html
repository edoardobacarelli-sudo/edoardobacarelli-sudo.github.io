ForecastingPlanned

Demand forecasting & inventory scenarios

Evaluate forecast accuracy and explore explicitly hypothetical inventory scenarios.

Dataset and final tools to be selected

Project scope

A proposed learning project using suitable historical demand data. The first task is selecting data with an appropriate time horizon, frequency and documented provenance.

Status & limitations

No dataset or model has been selected. Inventory scenarios will be hypothetical; simulated savings will not be presented as achieved business results. This is not a claim of professional forecasting experience.

Operation checklist

Expand each operation to inspect its intended scope. These are planned steps, not completed results.

1Select appropriate data+

Approve time coverage, granularity and source provenance.

2Audit the time series+

Check missing periods, anomalies and whether sales are a valid proxy for demand.

3Define the target and horizon+

Specify the quantity to forecast and the decision horizon.

4Set chronological evaluation periods+

Separate training and evaluation periods to prevent future information leakage.

5Build and compare forecasts+

Run a naive baseline and a suitable alternative on the same evaluation period.

6Evaluate errors and bias+

Use clearly defined measures to investigate accuracy and systematic over- or under-forecasting.

7Document scenario assumptions+

Explain inventory implications as hypothetical scenarios, with explicit assumptions and limitations.
