Project scope

Status & limitations

No analysis or experiment has been completed. A proposed test is not measured conversion uplift. Obfuscated sample data may have limited internal consistency.

Operation checklist

Expand each operation to inspect its intended scope. These are planned steps, not completed results.

1Confirm data and limitations+

Inspect the proposed Google sample and its obfuscation limitations.

2Define identities and funnel steps+

Specify users or sessions, event sequence and conversion eligibility rules.

3Audit event quality+

Check missing fields, duplicate events and consistency before analysis.

4Build the SQL funnel+

Prepare documented queries that count eligible progression through each step.

5Compare relevant segments+

Examine suitable segment differences without causal or attribution overclaims.

6Validate counts and denominators+

Reconcile stage counts and ensure comparisons use consistent definitions.

7Draft an experiment proposal+

Define a testable hypothesis, primary metric and guardrails. No uplift is claimed.
